# Entrenched

**Entrenched** is a brutal body-horror tabletop roleplaying game about military sappers trapped in an endless industrial war.

The unit crosses no-man's-land, maps what it discovers, completes impossible objectives, dies, and returns reconstructed from whatever flesh the war can recover. Every revival makes a sapper more capable and less human. Knowledge survives death, but identity does not.

> **Current status:** Open beta. The rules are playable, but balance, clarity, pacing, and presentation may change in response to testing.

## What You Need

- One referee and three to six players
- Several ordinary six-sided dice for checks and Humanity loss
- Printed character sheets
- A dark marker for permanently altering each sapper's record
- Grid paper, butcher paper, wrapping paper, or another long surface for the shared battlefield map
- Tokens or miniatures if your group wants them

Most operations are intended to last two to four sessions. A single run into no-man's-land should take roughly thirty minutes of play.

## The Core Loop

1. Receive an incomplete map and an objective worth more to command than the unit's lives.
2. Choose equipment and enter no-man's-land.
3. Explore, fight, and permanently mark discoveries on the shared map.
4. Provoke retaliation when checks fail or the unit hesitates.
5. Recover and reconstruct the dead—or abandon them to the battlefield.
6. Lose Humanity, gain replacement parts and skills, and physically alter the character sheet.
7. Return with better knowledge and fewer pieces of the people who first enlisted.

The operation ends when the objective is completed or the entire unit is permanently destroyed.

## Design Priorities

- Simple rules with lethal consequences
- Fast, dangerous combat
- Exploration that produces persistent knowledge
- Death as advancement rather than a reset
- Permanent physical changes to the character record
- Body horror built through accumulation, memory loss, and institutional cruelty
- Enemy soldiers, battlefield hazards, and monsters operating as one combined threat

## Content and Safety

Entrenched deals directly with:

- Graphic injury and surgical body horror
- Death, corpses, and involuntary reconstruction
- Memory loss and erosion of identity
- Dehumanizing military institutions
- War, captivity, execution, and mass death

These themes are central to the game, but graphic description is not mandatory. Read [Safety and Tone](https://techinthekitchen.github.io/Entrenched/?page=00%20Start%20Here%2FSafety%20and%20Tone.md) before play and agree on boundaries as a group.

## Beta Feedback

Useful playtest feedback includes:

- Number of players and session length
- How many runs and revivals occurred
- Whether checks and Resistance values were easy to judge
- Whether combat felt fast, understandable, and dangerous
- Which equipment choices felt essential, weak, or unclear
- Whether the shared map affected later decisions
- Whether Humanity loss and sheet alteration created the intended horror
- Any rule the group had to stop and interpret
- Moments that were especially tense, memorable, confusing, or slow

Please submit feedback through the repository's [Issues](https://github.com/TechInTheKitchen/Entrenched/issues) page.
