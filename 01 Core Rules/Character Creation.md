# Character Creation

Sappers begin as recruits: hastily trained, minimally equipped, and expected to learn their trade by dying. Every sapper begins as a Private with one specialty, seven total skill ranks, basic armor, and three equipment slots.

> [!NOTE] Printable Character Sheet
> Open or download the [[Entrenched Character Sheet.pdf|printable character sheet]] before beginning. Record every starting detail in ink. Keep a dark marker nearby; revival will overwrite the sapper's memories and alter the body diagram permanently.

## Starting Profile

- **Rank:** Private
- **Humanity:** 100
- **Specialty:** One
- **Skill ranks:** Seven total
- **Defense:** 2
- **Equipment slots:** Three, plus issued helmet and basic armor
- **Flesh boxes:** None until Humanity falls to 75

## 1. Record Enlistment Details

Record the person who entered service:

- Name
- Specialty
- Height and weight
- Date of birth
- Identifying marks
- A memory of life before the war
- A person, place, or belief they must not forget

Use ink. During play, Humanity rules will require the player to obscure and overwrite this information with a dark marker. Never erase it cleanly.

## 2. Choose a Specialty

A specialty is a starting package and guideline, not a permanent class. Choose one, gain 2 ranks in its listed skill, and learn its [[Specialties and Field Doctrines|Field Doctrine]].

| Specialty | Starting Skill | Role in the Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scout | Awareness 2 | Finds routes, hidden positions, movement, and danger before it finds the unit |
| Demolitionist | Demolitions 2 | Places charges and destroys structures, machines, and fortifications |
| Mine-Clearer | Engineering 2 | Identifies and neutralizes mines, traps, wire, and unexploded ordnance |
| Trench Fighter | Close Quarters 2 | Clears confined positions and protects the unit at arm's reach |
| Runner | Mobility 2 | Crosses exposed ground, carries orders, and reaches fallen sappers quickly |
| Rifleman | Ranged 2 | Engages distant threats before they can close on the unit |

The specialty name may be changed to fit the sapper. Its starting skill and Field Doctrine remain the same. A sapper keeps that doctrine even when later repairs and experience carry them far beyond their original role.

## 3. Assign Recruit Training

Assign 5 additional skill ranks among any skills. Each rank increases one chosen skill by 1. No skill may begin above rank 3 after specialty ranks and recruit training are combined. Skills have a maximum rank of 10.

Sappers gain skill ranks through revival. Broad skills leave room for specialization through equipment, scars, body parts, and the particular actions a sapper becomes known for.

## Skills

### Combat Skills

- **Ranged:** Rifles, pistols, machine guns, grenades, thrown equipment, and attacks beyond arm's reach.
- **Close Quarters:** Knives, clubs, bayonets, grappling, and fighting at Melee range.

### Field Skills

- **Awareness:** Finding enemies, hidden routes, mines, traps, distant movement, and battlefield changes.
- **Stealth:** Moving, hiding, and concealing bodies, equipment, or signs of passage.
- **Engineering:** Disarming mines and traps, cutting wire, sabotaging machines, repairing equipment, and building or placing traps.
- **Demolitions:** Placing explosives and controlling what their detonation destroys.

### Physical Skills

- **Strength:** Lifting, forcing, climbing, breaking, and other feats of power.
- **Mobility:** Running, balance, agility, reflexes, and escaping artillery, collapsing ground, fire, and shrapnel.

When an action is not clearly covered, the referee chooses the closest skill based on the sapper's approach. Rules for checks are in [[Skill Checks and Combat]].

## 4. Determine Rank

Rank follows Humanity. Command promotes reconstructed soldiers because their altered bodies can operate valuable equipment and because few will survive long enough to threaten the hierarchy.

| Rank | Humanity | Equipment Access | Equipment Slots |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Private | 100–76 | Level 1 | 3 |
| Corporal | 75–26 | Levels 1–2 | 4 |
| Sergeant | 25–1 | Levels 1–3 | 6 |
| Battleborn | 0 | Levels 1–3 | 6 |

Promotion occurs immediately when Humanity crosses a threshold. New equipment access and slots become available when the unit returns to the Allied trench before its next run. Rank never falls and Humanity cannot be restored.

Skill ranks show what the body has learned. Rank shows how close that body is to being expended.

## 5. Determine Defense and Flesh

An unarmored human has Defense 1. Every Private is issued a helmet and basic armor rated 1, giving a starting Defense of 2.

Basic armor does not consume an equipment slot. A Corporal may spend one slot on level 2 armor, and a Sergeant may spend one slot on level 3 armor. Improved armor replaces basic armor rather than adding to it.

Humanity does not increase Defense. Instead, at 75 Humanity a sapper gains one Flesh box, and at 25 Humanity they gain a second. A lethal hit marks an empty Flesh box and wounds the sapper. If none remain empty, the sapper becomes Dying, takes one Last Action on their next turn, and then dies.

## 6. Choose Equipment

Choose equipment from the [[Equipment]]. A sapper may choose items up to their rank's equipment level and may carry no more items than their available slots.

Armor, weapons, tools, explosives, and specialized supplies normally consume one slot each unless their description says otherwise. The issued helmet and basic armor do not consume slots.

## 7. Record Humanity

Every new sapper begins with 100 Humanity. Mark each threshold when crossed:

- 100–76: Human
- 75–51: Dead to Pain; Corporal; one Flesh box
- 50–26: Battlefield Intuition; three rerolls each run
- 25–1: Hardened Flesh; Sergeant; two Flesh boxes
- 0: Battleborn; three Burn boxes before permanent death

Full rules for identity loss, revival, Humanity, and Battleborn are in [[Death and Rebirth]].

## Ready for Deployment

Before play, confirm that the sheet shows:

- Enlistment details and two anchors to the sapper's former life
- One specialty and exactly seven total skill ranks
- Private rank, 100 Humanity, and Defense 2
- Three level 1 equipment choices
- Issued helmet and basic armor, which use no equipment slots
- No Flesh, Battlefield Intuition, or Burn boxes marked yet

Leave all ten zones of the body diagram and the replacement record untouched. They belong to the sapper's deaths, not their creation.
