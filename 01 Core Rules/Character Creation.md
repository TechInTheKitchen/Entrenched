# Character Creation

Sappers begin as recruits: hastily trained, minimally equipped, and expected to learn their trade by dying. Every sapper begins as a Private with one specialty, seven total skill ranks, basic armor, and three equipment slots.

> [!NOTE] Printable Character Sheet
> Open or download the [[Entrenched Character Sheet.pdf|printable character sheet]] before beginning. Record every starting detail in ink. Keep a dark marker nearby; revival will overwrite the sapper's memories and alter the body diagram permanently.

## Starting Profile

- **Rank:** Private
- **Humanity:** 100
- **Specialty:** One
- **Skill ranks:** Seven total
- **Defense:** 1
- **Equipment slots:** Three, plus issued helmet and basic armor
- **Wound boxes:** None until Humanity falls to 75

## 1. Record Enlistment Details

Record the person who entered service:

- Name
- Specialty
- Height and weight
- Date of birth
- Identifying marks
- A memory of life before the war
- A person, place, or belief they must not forget

Use ink. During play, Humanity rules will require the player to obscure and overwrite this information with a dark marker. Never erase it cleanly.

## 2. Choose a Specialty

A specialty is a starting package and guideline, not a permanent class. Choose one, gain 2 ranks in its listed skill, and learn its [[Character Creation#Field Doctrines|Field Doctrine]].

| Specialty | Starting Skill | Role in the Unit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scout | Awareness 2 | Finds routes, hidden positions, movement, and danger before it finds the unit |
| Demolitionist | Demolitions 2 | Places charges and destroys structures, machines, and fortifications |
| Mine-Clearer | Engineering 2 | Identifies and neutralizes mines, traps, wire, and unexploded ordnance |
| Trench Fighter | Close Quarters 2 | Clears confined positions and protects the unit at arm's reach |
| Runner | Mobility 2 | Crosses exposed ground, carries orders, and reaches fallen sappers quickly |
| Rifleman | Ranged 2 | Engages distant threats before they can close on the unit |

The specialty name may be changed to fit the sapper. Its starting skill and Field Doctrine remain the same. A sapper keeps that doctrine even when later repairs and experience carry them far beyond their original role.

## Field Doctrines

The chosen specialty grants one Field Doctrine. It remains available as the sapper's skills and body change; it is learned training, not a permanent class restriction.

Unless a doctrine says otherwise:

- A doctrine may be used once per run.
- Using a doctrine is part of the action or event that triggers it and does not cost another action.
- A doctrine cannot make an impossible action possible, reveal information the sapper could not perceive, attack an unseen target, or bypass the Protection of a [[Stationary Defenses|stationary defense]].
- A use is spent when its effect is declared. If it modifies a check, it is spent whether that check succeeds or fails.
- A doctrine refreshes at the beginning of the next run.

### Scout: Read the Ground

Once per run, before the unit enters a visible area, the Scout may study it and ask the referee one question:

- What is watching this area?
- What is the safest visible approach?
- What here has changed since the map was made?
- Where could one sapper remain unseen?

> [!NOTE] Referee Answer
> Answer honestly with what the Scout can reasonably infer from their position. The answer may reveal a danger, firing lane, sign, or opportunity without explaining everything concealed there or how it must be defeated. Asking the question requires enough time and visibility to examine the area; it cannot interrupt an attack or other retaliation already being resolved.

### Demolitionist: Leave the Right Ruin

Once per run, after succeeding on a Demolitions check, the Demolitionist chooses one additional benefit that follows from the blast:

>[!note] **A Demolitionist and partner are seen setting breaching charges on an enemy bunker**
>![[Sappers breaching a bunker.webp]]


- One named nearby person, route, or object is spared.
- The wreckage creates useful cover.
- The destruction appears accidental until closely inspected.
- One useful piece of salvage survives and can be recovered.

The choice cannot contradict the declared purpose of the charge. A blast intended to collapse the only bridge cannot also preserve that bridge, and controlled destruction cannot overcome a fortification that has not yet been made vulnerable.

### Mine-Clearer: Follow My Steps

Once per run, after identifying a trapped, mined, wired, or unstable route, the Mine-Clearer may lead the unit through it. Make one Engineering check for the crossing.

On a success, every sapper who follows the Mine-Clearer's exact path as part of the same crossing passes through safely. On a failure, the crossing does not succeed and the referee makes one primary retaliation against the exposed unit rather than calling for a separate check or retaliation for every sapper.

This doctrine does not disarm the hazard or make the route permanently safe. Mark it as known but still dangerous on the shared map. Anyone who leaves the path or crosses it later faces the hazard normally.

### Trench Fighter: Behind Me

Once per run, when an adjacent sapper is selected as the target of an attack, the Trench Fighter may intervene before the attack is rolled.

If the attack has only one target, the Trench Fighter becomes its target. If the same attack already threatens both sappers, the protected sapper instead gains +1 Defense against that attack; the Trench Fighter gains no Defense. Resolve every other target normally.

The Trench Fighter must be able to perceive the attack and physically place their body between it and the protected sapper. This doctrine cannot intercept gas filling an area, collapsing ground, or another effect for which one body provides no protection.

### Runner: No One Left

Once per run, when another sapper becomes Dying, the Runner may immediately move up to their normal movement toward that sapper. Resolve this movement after the effect that made the sapper Dying and before play continues.

Every square crossed must bring the Runner closer by a viable route, and the movement must end closer to the fallen sapper. Dangerous ground still requires the normal check and may stop the Runner or provoke retaliation. Reaching the sapper does not include treatment, revival, an attack, or another free action.

### Rifleman: Wait for the Opening

The Rifleman may spend an action aiming at one visible position, route, or opening. Mark the Rifleman as **Aimed**. Their next Ranged attack against a target in that position reduces that target's Defense by 1, to a minimum of 1.

Unlike other doctrines, Wait for the Opening has no per-run limit because aiming costs an action. The Rifleman ceases to be Aimed if they move, attack another target, lose sight of the chosen position, or become Dying. The doctrine cannot attack an unseen target, ignore range, or bypass the Protection of a stationary defense. It may exploit an Opening that the unit has already created.

### Changing a Specialty Name

The player may rename a specialty to fit the sapper's history while retaining its skill and doctrine. A Scout might be called an Observer, a Runner a Stretcher-Bearer, or a Trench Fighter a Raider. The title changes; the rules do not.

## 3. Assign Recruit Training

Assign 5 additional skill ranks among any skills. Each rank is one checked box on that skill's row. Combined with the 2 ranks from specialty, every recruit begins with 7 checked boxes total. No skill may begin above rank 3; over a sapper's lifetime, no skill may exceed rank 12.

Sappers gain additional **skill ranks** through revival. Check one more box in the chosen skill for each rank gained. The number of checked boxes is the skill's current rank and the number of skill dice it contributes. Broad skills leave room for specialization through equipment, scars, body parts, and the particular actions a sapper becomes known for.

## Skills

### Combat Skills

- **Ranged:** Rifles, pistols, machine guns, grenades, thrown equipment, and attacks beyond arm's reach.
- **Close Quarters:** Knives, clubs, bayonets, grappling, and fighting at Melee range.

### Field Skills

- **Awareness:** Finding enemies, hidden routes, mines, traps, distant movement, and battlefield changes.
- **Stealth:** Moving, hiding, and concealing bodies, equipment, or signs of passage.
- **Engineering:** Disarming mines and traps, cutting wire, sabotaging machines, repairing equipment, and building or placing traps.
- **Demolitions:** Placing explosives and controlling what their detonation destroys.

### Physical Skills

- **Strength:** Lifting, forcing, climbing, breaking, and other feats of power.
- **Mobility:** Running, balance, agility, reflexes, and escaping artillery, collapsing ground, fire, and shrapnel.

When an action is not clearly covered, the referee chooses the closest skill based on the sapper's approach. Rules for checks are in [[Skill Checks and Exploration]].

## 4. Determine Rank

Rank follows Humanity. Command promotes reconstructed soldiers because their altered bodies can operate valuable equipment and because few will survive long enough to threaten the hierarchy.

| Rank | Humanity | Equipment Access | Equipment Slots |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Private | 100–76 | Level 1 | 3 |
| Corporal | 75–26 | Levels 1–2 | 4 |
| Sergeant | 25–1 | Levels 1–3 | 6 |
| Battleborn | 0 | Levels 1–3 | 6 |

Promotion occurs immediately when Humanity crosses a threshold. New equipment access and slots become available when the unit returns to the Allied trench before its next run. Rank never falls and Humanity cannot be restored.

Checked skill ranks show both the recruit's training and what the body learned after enlistment. Military rank shows how close that body is to being expended.

## 5. Determine Defense and Wounds

Every new sapper has **Defense 1**, even in an issued helmet and basic armor. At 75 Humanity the body can survive one lethal hit through Wounds, but its Defense remains 1. Once Humanity falls below 75, accumulated reconstruction, scar tissue, and altered reflexes raise the sapper's base Defense to 2.

Basic armor does not consume an equipment slot and is already included in the starting profile. Improved armor replaces the issued protection and modifies Defense as described in [[Equipment]] rather than adding to it.

This change is not promotion armor. It is the body's growing difficulty in accepting a clean death. At 75 Humanity a sapper gains one Wound box, and at 25 Humanity they gain a second. A lethal hit marks an empty Wound box and wounds the sapper. If none remain empty, the sapper becomes Dying, takes one Last Action on their next turn, and then dies.

Use the sheet's Wound spaces to show how many boxes the sapper has gained, and red paper clips on the sheet edge to show which boxes are currently marked by wounds. See [[Death and Revival#Tracking Wounds at the Table|Tracking Wounds at the Table]].

## 6. Choose Equipment

Choose equipment from the [[Equipment]]. A sapper may choose items up to their rank's equipment level and may carry no more items than their available slots.

Armor, weapons, tools, explosives, and specialized supplies normally consume one slot each unless their description says otherwise. The issued helmet and basic armor do not consume slots.

## 7. Record Humanity

Every new sapper begins with 100 Humanity. Mark each threshold when crossed:

- 100–76: Human
- 75–51: Pain-Dead; Corporal; one Wound box
- 50–26: Borrowed Reflex; one reroll each run
- 25–1: Redundant Anatomy; Sergeant; two Wound boxes
- 0: Battleborn; three Burn boxes before becoming Burn-Listed

Revival and recovery are in [[Death and Revival]]. Identity loss, thresholds, and Burn are in [[Humanity and Battleborn]].

## Ready for Deployment

Before play, confirm that the sheet shows:

- Enlistment details and two anchors to the sapper's former life
- One specialty and exactly seven total skill ranks
- Private rank, 100 Humanity, and Defense 1
- Three level 1 equipment choices
- Issued helmet and basic armor, which use no equipment slots
- No Wounds, or Burn marked yet

Leave all ten zones of the body diagram and the replacement record untouched. They belong to the sapper's deaths, not their creation.
