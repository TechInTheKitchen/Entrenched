# The Enemy

Allied doctrine refers to opposing soldiers only as **the enemy**. National names are absent from briefings. Captured maps are translated before sappers see them. Prisoners are numbered, masked, and removed quickly.

This is presented as operational security. Its true purpose is to prevent recognition.

## What Sappers See

Enemy soldiers wear unfamiliar respirators, layered armor, and uniforms faded into the same mud as Allied cloth. Their weapons are different enough to be threatening and similar enough to operate without instruction. Their reconstructed veterans display animal grafts, borrowed Allied parts, and living equipment shaped by another surgical tradition.

They recover their dead. They mark routes. They send sappers through no-man's-land. They burn Battleborn.

The enemy should be frightening because it is capable and prepared, not because every soldier is a monster.

## What Command Says

- The enemy began the Long War.
- The enemy has no civilian population, only breeding districts.
- Enemy revival produces obedient copies without souls.
- Enemy soldiers cannot surrender sincerely.
- Their language contains no word for peace.

Captured evidence contradicts all five claims.

## Referee Truth

The enemy is another fortified coalition held together by conscription, state religion, and fear of Allied invasion. Its soldiers receive almost identical instruction about the Allies. They call themselves defenders. Their reconstruction program descends from the same Eisenseele procedures.

This does not make them harmless. Enemy officers protect their trench with the same calculated brutality Allied command uses. Human similarity should complicate the horror, not remove danger.

## Revealing the Mirror

Use small evidence rather than speeches:

- An enemy family photograph posed before a familiar monument
- A prayer translated word for word into the Allied burial rite
- A ration tin manufactured in a supposedly Allied city
- An enemy sapper carrying a grease-pencil map covered in death marks
- A replacement hand that salutes an enemy officer automatically
- A prisoner terrified that Allied Butchers will steal their memories
- An enemy red signal flag with names written along its hem
- Orders referring to the players as corpse-thieves and soulless copies

Do not require the players to sympathize or negotiate. The discovery should make killing intimate and propaganda unstable.

## Enemy Reconstruction

Enemy reconstruction tends to be more visibly integrated with equipment. Respirators may be grafted into lungs, rifles grown along forearms, and signal wire threaded through the spine. Allied observers call this proof of enemy inhumanity. Enemy observers describe Allied patchwork bodies as uncontrolled contamination.

When the enemy recovers an Allied body, choose a purpose:

- Study its memories and learned routes
- Use recognizable parts to bait the unit
- Rebuild it as a prisoner or infiltrator
- Incorporate useful tissue into an enemy veteran
- Burn it to deny Allied recovery
- Return it deliberately with a message stitched inside

## Keeping the Enemy Unnamed

Players may learn personal names, unit numbers, and local words. The larger coalition can remain unnamed. No single label can contain thirty-seven years of collapsed nations and merged commands.

If the players demand a proper name, give them several mutually incompatible names from maps, prisoners, sermons, and old documents. Every faction believes its own name means the Allied Defense.
