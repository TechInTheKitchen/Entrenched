# Faith of the Final Trench

The churches of the prewar world did not survive unchanged. Their buildings became hospitals, recruitment halls, corpse depots, and artillery landmarks. Their languages and symbols were absorbed into the state doctrine now called the **Faith of the Final Trench**.

The Faith teaches that the world above is only the forward line of a greater war. The worthy dead continue serving in trenches beneath creation until final victory. Revival is therefore not resurrection but temporary reassignment.

## Common Teachings

- Pain proves the soul remains attached to useful flesh.
- Borrowed parts are reinforcements sent by the honored dead.
- Forgotten memories have been transferred to the Final Trench for safekeeping.
- Battleborn stand with one foot in each battlefield.
- Permanent incineration releases a loyal soul from further earthly duty.

These teachings make reconstruction bearable and military losses sacred. They also make refusal a form of spiritual treason.

## Chaplains

Chaplains wear gold because it is the color believed visible from the other side. Their vestments are cut to fit over armor and replacement limbs. They witness Butcher procedures, preserve original names, calm living weapons, and decide whether a speaking reconstruction is legally the soldier named on its tags.

Some chaplains are sincere comforts in an unbearable system. Others are political officers with censers. Most are both.

## Rites

### Before a Run

The chaplain touches the red flag to every sapper and says, “Return by the road you make.” The unit answers, “Mark it behind us.”

### Before Reconstruction

The original name is spoken three times: once to the body, once to the donor material, and once into a field telephone. If the telephone answers, the Butcher records which voice spoke.

### Permanent Death

The dead sapper's remaining name is written on flash paper and burned. If the flame turns black, the congregation does not discuss it.

## Heresies

- **The Empty Return:** The belief that revival produces a new being and the original remains trapped below.
- **The Merciful Flame:** Secret groups who incinerate recoverable bodies to spare them further service.
- **The One Soul:** The claim that every revived soldier contains fragments of a single vast dead consciousness.
- **The Peace Below:** The forbidden teaching that the Final Trench contains no war until the living carry it there through revival.

Any of these beliefs can motivate an NPC without being objectively correct.
