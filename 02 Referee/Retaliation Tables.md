# Retaliation Tables

When a check fails or the unit hesitates, the battlefield retaliates. The referee may choose a fitting result or roll `d20` on the most relevant table.

Retaliation follows the fiction. Ignore or reinterpret results that cannot happen in the current position. Shelter may prevent bombardment, an empty minefield cannot produce an enemy mine, and the enemy cannot react with knowledge nobody survived to report.

These tables are prompts, not limits. A result may threaten one sapper or the whole unit depending on who is exposed.

## Artillery and Mortars

| d20 | Retaliation |
| ---: | --- |
| 1 | A ranging shell lands nearby. Mark the square; the next artillery result centers there unless the unit moves. |
| 2 | The blast throws every exposed sapper 2 squares in different directions. |
| 3 | Dense smoke and earth erase sight beyond Close range until the unit moves. |
| 4 | A dud buries itself nearby. Mark it as unexploded ordnance with Resistance 2 to defuse. |
| 5 | Shrapnel tears open a carried item; destroy one exposed consumable use. |
| 6 | Flying metal marks one sapper's Flesh box; if none is empty, they become Dying. |
| 7 | The ground collapses beneath one sapper, trapping them until a Resistance 1 Strength or Mobility check succeeds. |
| 8 | A body is thrown 6 squares from its marked location. |
| 9 | A shelter entrance collapses and must be cleared at Resistance 2. |
| 10 | The bombardment reveals a buried entrance, body, cache, or Black Matter seam. |
| 11 | Walking fire crosses the unit's route; every exposed sapper must move immediately or mark a Flesh box, becoming Dying if none is empty. |
| 12 | One sapper's equipment is buried. Mark it; recovering it requires an action under continued fire. |
| 13 | A mortar crew brackets the unit. Until silenced or escaped, hesitation retaliates twice. |
| 14 | A direct hit destroys a marked piece of shelter after its occupants escape or mark a Flesh box, becoming Dying if none is empty. |
| 15 | The concussion attracts a Feral Corpse Hound. |
| 16 | A nearby enemy patrol uses the barrage to advance from Far range. |
| 17 | One exposed sapper is killed by shrapnel. |
| 18 | The closest unrecovered body is pulped; its next field revival rolls twice on the scar table and keeps both scars. |
| 19 | A heavy shell kills every sapper in one chosen square. |
| 20 | The map changes: create a crater and destroy everything in a 2-square area, then mark the new terrain. |

## Scaling Retaliation

Early in an operation, favor consequences that reveal information, alter position, or create threats. As the sappers learn the map and grow stronger, use results that kill, capture bodies, destroy equipment, and deploy reconstructed enemies.

Do not protect a sapper merely because they have not died recently. Do not kill them arbitrarily when a more specific consequence would make the map or next decision more interesting. Brutality works best when players can understand what killed them and carry that knowledge into the next run.
