# Enemies and Monsters

No-man's-land contains enemy soldiers, reconstructed veterans, living weapons, and things assembled by accident from everything the war left unburied.

The enemy is mechanically comparable to the sappers. They use the same dice, ranges, actions, Defense, and lethal combat rules. A single enemy soldier can kill a sapper. The war rarely sends only one.

## Reading an Enemy Profile

Each profile lists:

- **Defense:** Successes required to kill it.
- **Speed:** Squares it can move with one action.
- **Attack:** Dice rolled and the attack's range.
- **Skills:** Dice used for important non-combat checks.
- **Traits:** Rules unique to the enemy.

Weapon dice are already included in listed attack pools. The referee does not need to build the pool again.

## Enemy Soldiers

Enemy soldiers can use more detailed skills and equipment when a mission requires it. The following profiles keep ordinary fights quick.

### Line Soldier

**Defense:** 2  
**Speed:** 4  
**Attack:** Rifle 4 dice at Far; bayonet 3 dice at Melee  
**Skills:** Awareness 3, Mobility 3, Strength 2

Line soldiers hold firing positions, patrol known routes, and concentrate fire when directed. They are roughly equal to newly enlisted sappers but usually fight from prepared positions and in greater numbers.

### Enemy Sapper

**Defense:** 2  
**Speed:** 4  
**Attack:** Trench gun 4 dice at Close; knife 3 dice at Melee  
**Skills:** Choose two mission-relevant skills at 5 dice and other checks at 2 dice

Enemy sappers lay mines, recover bodies, alter routes, and investigate Allied equipment. Give each patrol a purpose such as mining a breach, retrieving remains, repairing wire, or hunting the party.

### Assault Corporal

**Defense:** 3  
**Speed:** 6  
**Attack:** Automatic trench gun 7 dice at Close; pile-driver 6 dice at Melee  
**Skills:** Awareness 5, Mobility 5, Strength 5

An Assault Corporal directs nearby soldiers. While the Corporal lives, enemy soldiers on the same map may act in whichever order best supports one another.

### Enemy Sergeant

**Defense:** 4  
**Speed:** 8  
**Attack:** Choose a level 3 weapon; 10 dice at its listed range  
**Skills:** Three specialties at 8 dice; other trained actions at 5 dice

Enemy Sergeants are reconstructed veterans carrying rare war technology. They recognize revival kits, target body-recovery efforts, and retreat when preserving their experience will hurt the sappers more than dying.

### Enemy Battleborn

**Defense:** 6  
**Speed:** 10  
**Attack:** 12 dice at one listed range; 9 dice at another  
**Skills:** Relevant checks at 10 dice

An enemy Battleborn revives without assistance unless it is completely immobilized or its remains are incinerated. It remembers the party between runs and may incorporate unrecovered Allied parts into its body.

A Battleborn should be a mission problem rather than an incidental encounter. Trapping, immobilizing, and burning it is more reliable than repeatedly winning direct combat.

## Enemy Recovery

Enemy recovery details operate between runs. Unless the sappers prevent it, enemy bodies are removed, reconstructed, and returned to service. A marked patrol route may therefore be occupied again even after the party kills everyone assigned to it.

The sappers can interfere by:

- Burning or destroying bodies
- Trapping bodies and recovery routes
- Dragging enemy remains away
- Collapsing access trenches
- Destroying corpse depots and Butcher workshops
- Using enemy remains for field revival

Enemies can do the same to Allied bodies. A sapper captured intact may return with enemy alterations, be used as bait, or be incinerated if already Battleborn.

# Monsters of No-Man's-Land

Monsters are not a separate ecosystem. They are consequences: corpses combined by shelling, repair organisms that escaped, bodies that revived without enough memory to remain people, and Black Matter teaching meat how to move.

### Feral Corpse Hound

**Defense:** 2  
**Speed:** 8  
**Attack:** Bite 5 dice at Melee  
**Skills:** Awareness 6, Stealth 4

Corpse hounds are bred to find and recover bodies. Allied hounds recognize regiment scents and are normally harmless. Feral or enemy hounds drag fallen sappers toward their own trench. A hound that begins its turn beside a body may drag it its full Speed.

### Patchwork Heap

**Defense:** 3  
**Speed:** 2  
**Attack:** Grasping limbs 6 dice at Melee  
**Skills:** Strength 8, Awareness 2

Dozens of partial bodies share one directionless revival. When beside a corpse at the beginning of its turn, the Heap incorporates it, gains 1 Defense for the rest of the run, and removes the body from play until the Heap is killed.

### Skinless Burrower

**Defense:** 2  
**Speed:** 10  
**Attack:** Eruption 6 dice at Melee  
**Skills:** Stealth 7 beneath earth, Awareness 5

The Burrower travels through soft ground and corpse-filled mud. Its position cannot be attacked until it erupts or an Awareness check reveals its moving tunnel. Substantial flooring, flooded ground, and old foundations block it.

### Wire Saint

**Defense:** 4  
**Speed:** 2  
**Attack:** Barbed embrace 7 dice at Close  
**Skills:** Awareness 5, Strength 8

Bodies have grown through a mass of signal wire and barbed coils. A sapper killed by its attack is pulled adjacent to it. At the beginning of its next turn, the Saint knots the body into itself; recovering that sapper then requires killing the Saint or passing a Resistance 3 Engineering check beside it.

### Trench Mare

**Defense:** 3  
**Speed:** 12  
**Attack:** Charge 7 dice at Melee  
**Skills:** Mobility 7, Awareness 5, Strength 7

A long equine reconstruction burdened with human arms and respirators. If it moves at least 6 squares before attacking, add 2 dice to its charge. After a failed attack, it continues moving in a straight line until blocked.

### Choir in the Mud

**Defense:** 2  
**Speed:** 0  
**Attack:** Remembered scream 6 dice at Close  
**Skills:** Awareness 8

Only its mouths break the surface. When the Choir detects a sapper, it imitates Allied voices, names, and calls for help. If not silenced before the next enemy turn, its screaming triggers an artillery retaliation centered on its position.

### Black Matter Bloom

**Defense:** 3  
**Speed:** 0  
**Attack:** Rooted tendrils 7 dice at Close  
**Skills:** Awareness 7 through connected earth

The Bloom grows where too many revivals have soaked the same ground. A sapper killed beside it is pulled below the surface. If not recovered before the next run, the body returns as an enemy reconstruction with one Black Matter replacement and no memory of its unit.

### The Unreturned

**Defense:** 2  
**Speed:** 6  
**Attack:** Stolen weapon 5 dice at its proper range  
**Skills:** Stealth 6, Awareness 4, one remembered specialty at 6

The Unreturned resemble missing sappers until light catches the seams. They remember names, routes, and fragments of unit procedure. They attempt to separate one sapper from the group before attacking and may carry equipment lost during previous runs.

## Building New Horrors

Start with a recognizable battlefield function: recovery, concealment, breaching, observation, transport, or denial of ground. Give it a body assembled from the war's remains, then add one rule that changes how the sappers approach it.

Use these profiles as rough benchmarks:

| Threat | Defense | Attack Dice | Speed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Lesser horror | 2 | 4–5 | 4–8 |
| Serious horror | 3 | 6–7 | 2–10 |
| Major horror | 4 | 8–10 | 2–12 |
| Operation threat | 5+ | 10+ | Any |

Numbers alone do not make a memorable monster. Its behavior should threaten bodies, map knowledge, equipment, or the unit's ability to recover its dead.
